# 封神．人間劫 | Fengshen: Human Calamity

> 「她造了人類，給了人類自由意志。人類用那個自由意志，做了她看見的一切。」
> *"She created humans and gave them free will. They used that free will to do everything she witnessed."*

---

## 專案定性 | What is this?

長期世界觀型 AI 動畫計畫。

以**商朝巫祭文明**為舞台，從商王殷壽與姜皇后之間的婚姻裂縫出發，透過女媧對人性的失望、封神事件的起源，描繪一個文明在神性與人性之間崩壞的群像劇。

A long-form mythology animation project set in the **Bronze Age Shang Dynasty**.

Beginning from a fracture in the marriage between King Yin Shou and Queen Jiang — and tracing how that fracture sets in motion events that will reshape the boundary between the divine and the human.

**非爽劇。無英雄時刻。沒有人是錯的，所有人都在失去。**
**No epic heroes. No clear villains. No one is wrong. Everyone is losing something.**

---

## 核心命題 | Core Themes

- 封神不是正邪大戰，是一場被漸漸默許的人間悲劇
- 歷史記憶永遠由勝者書寫——誰書寫誰就擁有了定義權
- 神明的困惑，比人類的痛苦更難承受

*Fengshen is not a war between good and evil. It is a human tragedy that heaven chose not to stop.*
*History is always written by those who survive.*
*A god's confusion may be heavier than any human grief.*

## 核心美學 | Aesthetic

- **色調**：黑金青銅，消光，無高飽和色 / Black, gold, bronze — matte, no saturated color
- **光源**：火光、燭光、月光——光是稀缺的 / Firelight, candlelight, moonlight — light is rare
- **文明底色**：商朝巫祭（非修仙、非仙俠）/ Shang ritual civilization — not cultivation / xianxia
- **氛圍**：壓抑、潮濕、神性與人性的撕裂感 / Heavy, damp, the tearing sensation between divinity and humanity

**風格參考**：《沙丘》、《權力遊戲》前期、《甄嬛傳》、《銀翼殺手2049》（空氣感）
*Style references: Dune, Game of Thrones (early seasons), Empresses in the Palace, Blade Runner 2049 (atmosphere)*

## 世界觀基礎 | World Setting

**商朝不是「壞的朝代」，是人類文明最接近神性的時刻。**

- 商王既是世俗君主，也是巫王——天地之間的仲介者
- 神明的存在是人類集體信仰的凝聚，而非全能的主宰
- 封神，不是懲罰，是一種文明形式的終結與重整

*The Shang Dynasty was not a corrupt civilization — it was the moment when human civilization stood closest to the divine.*
*The gods exist because humans believe in them. When that belief fractures, something far larger begins to move.*
